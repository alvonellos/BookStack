====== What this book is not ======

This book is not a typical IT textbook filled with dry, technical jargon or purely theoretical concepts. It's not a step-by-step guide to certifications or a manual for passing exams. It also isn't a traditional memoir in the sense of just telling a story for the sake of storytelling. Rather, it’s a fusion of personal experiences, real-life struggles, technical knowledge, and philosophical reflections that speak to both the practical and the human side of a career in IT. It’s not just for aspiring IT professionals but for anyone who’s navigating challenges, fighting personal battles, or looking for inspiration to keep going. It isn’t meant to be easy to digest—it’s raw, unique, and packed with insights, not just information.
