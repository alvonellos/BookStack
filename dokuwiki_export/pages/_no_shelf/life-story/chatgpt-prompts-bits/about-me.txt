====== About ME ======

My name’s Alex Alvonellos, and if there’s one thing you need to know about me—it’s that I don’t quit. I’ve walked through fire more than once, and each time I came out sharper, faster, and more focused. I speak quick, think quicker, and carry a voice shaped by two worlds: the Southern United States and four years in England. What comes out is a blend of drawl and discipline, wit and grit.
I’ve lived everywhere and nowhere, grown up around code, chaos, and contradiction. My life hasn’t followed a straight line, but I’ve made meaning from the mess. I’m autistic, resilient, and wired to solve hard problems with a mix of logic and raw persistence. I learn by doing, and I refuse to accept the way things are when I can see the way they could be.
I’ve rebuilt myself more than once—after a crash, after loss, after failure—and each time, I came back with more clarity and purpose. I work in tech, but I move like a philosopher. Every line of code, every project, every idea I touch is driven by something deeper: a hunger for truth, a refusal to be silenced, and a love for building systems that are hard to break.
I don’t bluff. I don’t beg. I speak fire in a whisper and truth in a tongue you can’t quite place. And whatever room I walk into—I’m not just there to play the game. I’m there to change it.
That’s me. Alex.
