====== Reflection ======

As I look back on that year, I see it as a bridge between who I was and who I am becoming. It was the year the walls closed in—and the year I started learning how to push them back.
I still live with reminders of what happened. Scars on my skin. Memories that sometimes sneak up and steal my breath. An instinctive flinch when certain noises crack through the air. But I also live with a deeper understanding of myself, a resilience forged in places I never wanted to go.
I know now that healing isn’t about erasing the past. It’s about carrying it forward with grace. It’s about choosing, every day, to keep going even when the road ahead looks impossible. It’s about letting scars be reminders of survival rather than just of pain.
I’ve learned to let go of the idea that life has to look perfect to be worth living. My life is messy, complicated, and sometimes unbearably hard. But it’s also full of moments that take my breath away—the sun breaking through clouds, my husband’s laughter, the weight of a pen in my hand as I write my story.
And so I keep moving forward. Not because the journey is easy, but because it’s mine. Because I believe there’s still beauty waiting for me on the other side of all this.
Never give up. Better for being broken.
