====== Tolerance ======

Over time, I started noticing changes I hadn’t expected. My tolerance for small talk vanished. I craved deeper conversations—the kind where people admitted they were struggling, too. I didn’t want polite smiles. I wanted honesty, vulnerability, and connection.
I also became fiercely protective of my own energy. I learned to leave situations that felt unsafe, even if that meant disappointing people. I realized that my survival mattered more than anyone’s expectations.
Music became a refuge for me. Certain songs felt like lifelines, their lyrics echoing things I couldn’t always say out loud. I’d play the same tracks over and over, using the rhythm to drown out my racing thoughts.
There were breakthroughs, quiet but powerful. Like the day I walked into a store and didn’t feel my chest tighten. Or the morning I woke up and realized I hadn’t dreamed about the crash for three nights in a row. Small victories, but they mattered more than anyone could know.
Yet, I also felt how permanent some scars were. I’d catch myself scanning parking lots for danger, even on calm days. I’d listen for patterns in noises, waiting for some hidden threat to reveal itself. Some wounds, I realized, would never disappear entirely.
Still, I was building a life again, piece by fragile piece. A life where joy could exist beside grief, and fear didn’t always win. A life shaped not only by what had broken me—but by how I chose to rise afterward.
And in the quiet moments, I held onto my truth: Never give up. Better for being broken.
